01.About

Hi, I'm Hua — I also go by Edward. I took an unusual road into software: I trained in medicine at Xiangya, then studied epidemiology at Yale, where I learned to look past individual patients to the patterns in whole populations.

Years in health data science taught me how much rides on the systems underneath the analysis, so I went back to Penn for a master's in computer science and became an engineer. What ties it all together is the work I enjoy most: turning messy, high-stakes data into systems people can rely on.
